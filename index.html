About

Plenty of people learn to ride, stop for twenty years, and then find themselves standing in a dealership wondering what happened to motorcycles. Second Kickstart is for them.

The coverage is adventure and dual-sport machines — the bikes that get ridden to the trailhead and then down it. Model reviews, ranked comparisons, and the practical material nobody puts in a brochure: what a bike actually weighs when it is loaded, what breaks, what it costs to keep, and what to pack for a week away from pavement.

Reviews are written for the rider who is spending their own money and has to live with the decision.

Editorial standards

Rankings are not for sale

No manufacturer, dealer, or advertiser pays for placement in a ranking or a favorable review. If a bike is supplied, an event is hosted, or a video is sponsored, that is disclosed on screen and in the description.

Manufacturer assets are credited

Press imagery and footage are used under editorial permission, credited on screen to the manufacturer and again in the video description, and never altered or used in advertising.

No implied endorsement

Using a manufacturer's press materials does not mean that manufacturer endorses this channel, its reviews, or where its bikes land in a ranking.

Corrections

Specification errors and factual mistakes are corrected in the video description and acknowledged in a pinned comment. Write to the press address and it gets fixed.
